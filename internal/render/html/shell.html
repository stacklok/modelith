Domain model

Click a node or press Enter or Space to see details and highlight its neighbors. Use the Entity details panel's Collapse button to give the graph more room; its Details rail reopens it, and selecting nodes still updates collapsed details. Hover or click a relationship, or focus its label with Tab, to highlight both endpoints; move away or Tab out to return to the selection. Press Escape in the graph to clear the selected or hovered relationship highlight. Search dims nonmatching entities even when selected or highlighted; clear the search to see all. Drag nodes to move them; focus a node and press Alt+arrow keys to move it by 40 units. Drag the canvas or use arrow keys to pan; + and − zoom, 0 fits the view. Choose a layout and press Arrange to replace manual positions. Moves, selection, search, and theme last only until reload.

Arrows point from source to target · solid: owned · dashed: referenced · purple: is-a · dotted outline: external. Self-relationships are listed on nodes and in entity details. When an edge is obscured or has no clear short label connection, labels below the graph read Source → Target · role · cardinality, without a stem.
